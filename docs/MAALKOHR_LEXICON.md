---

## PREFACE

The Máalkohr language was not invented. It was recovered.

Someone began building a world. They wrote 138 words in a spreadsheet without knowing what they were building. A language emerged — zero wasted morphemes, a complete moral taxonomy encoded in phonology. The alignment system was not a filing tag. It was theology.

Every word in Máalkohr carries its alignment like a brand. When the gods imposed their moral taxonomy on the world, they did it through language first. The Wilak did not choose to speak a language that called family evil and purposeful killing righteous. The language was given to them. The language IS the Aleabishal's first law.

The grammar extends from 11 rules to 30. The vocabulary grows from 138 roots to over 350. Every word here is consistent with the base phonology, morphological rules, and alignment system.

**How to use this document:**
- `[alignment]` codes: LG, NG, CG, LN, TN, CN, LE, NE, CE
- `[root]` = a base morpheme
- `[compound]` = built from roots using apostrophe rules
- `[proper]` = deity or place name
- `[taboo]` = spoken only in whispers; some dialects omit entirely
- Words from MALKOHR_LINGUISTICS.md are marked `[existing]`

### TIER 1 — WORD FORMATION (Rules 1–11, summarized)

**Rule 1 — Compound Formation**
`[Root1]'[Root2]` = compound. Apostrophe marks every semantic join.
`Johr'ulf` = Earth-Wolf. `Haitt'ok` = War-Walker.

**Rule 2 — Body Extension (-me)**
`[BodyPart] + me` = "that which extends from [BodyPart]."
`Duur` (foot) → `Duurme` (leg). `Johr` (earth) → `Johrme` (deep foundation).

**Rule 3 — Agent Suffix (-ok)**
`[Root]'ok` = "one who embodies/does the root."
`Johr'ok` = Earth-Walker. `Haitt'ok` = Warrior. `Biik'ok` = Bone-Walker.

**Rule 4 — Place Suffix (-ia / -vellia)**
`[Concept] + ia` = land/place of. `Kohrvellia` = Land of the Choosing-Captains.

**Rule 5 — Divine Name Formation**
`[Concept1-truncated]'[Concept2-modified]['suffix]` = divine name.
`Ulfkon'ur` = Wolf-Chosen-Collective. `Uba'niyata` = Bird-Death.

**Rule 6 — Number System (-ki / cois)**
`[1–9] + ki` = 10 + number (teens). `[Number]-cois` = number × 10.
`al` (1), `uwa` (2), `ina` (3), `apral` (4), `ekvai/ekva` (5), `izol` (6), `kolo` (7), `ujwa` (8), `laibi` (9), `cois` (10), `uki` (11), `uwki` (12).

**Rule 7 — Phonological Alignment Markers**
| Sound | Alignment | Domain |
|-------|-----------|--------|
| `V-` | NG / CG | Body, institution, governance |
| `k'-` | LG or CE | Sacred force (holy or destructive extreme) |
| `ch'-` | LN / CN | Cultural identity, forbidden knowledge |
| `naa'` | — | Divine maternal (sacred prefix) |
| Short CVC | TN | Numbers, primal forces |
| Long `t`-cluster compounds | LE / NE | Power, war, suffering |

**Rule 8 — Sacred vs. Mortal Plural**
`root + e` (no apostrophe) = mortal plural. `Wilak → Wilake`.
`root + 'e` (with apostrophe) = sacred/divine plural. `Skaer → Skaer'e`.

**Rule 9 — Ordinal Prefix**
Numbers truncate and sometimes transform to form ordinals before title roots.
`al → Alea-`, `uwa → Uwa-`, `ina → Inra-`, `apral → Apra-`, `ekvai → Ekva-`.

**Rule 10 — Active Pursuit Suffix (-eitla)**
`[Root] + eitla` = the systematic, active pursuit of the root concept.
`nolu + eitla` = `nolueitla` (scientific investigation — a capital crime in Kohrsâra).

**Rule 11 — Divine Genitive ('il)**
`'il` = sacred "of" — possession by something divine.
`hei'il` = the divine grace that belongs to a god. `prach'ilskae` = rank-of-god.

### TIER 2 — NEGATION & MODIFICATION (Rules 12–15)

**Rule 12 — Negation Prefix (vel')**
`vel'[concept]` = the refusal of that concept; its commanded absence.
`vel'kohr` = the unchosen (the one not selected, or the refusal to select).
`vel'hei` = not-grace (what remains when the divine gift is withdrawn).
`vel'nolu` = unknowing (the deliberate absence of knowledge).
Note: `vel'` and `vel` (captain/lead) are distinguished by context. `vel'kohr` = refusal-of-choice; `vel'ok` = the leading-agent. When ambiguous, surrounding words resolve meaning.

**Rule 13 — Causative Suffix (-ilak)**
`[Root]-ilak` = "that which causes [root] in others."
`haitt'ilak` = battle-commander (causes war in others).
`naetuk'ilak` = the terrorizer (causes fear in others).
`kohr'ilak` = the one who forces choice upon others.
Distinct from `-ok` (one who does) — `-ilak` is the one who makes others do.

**Rule 14 — Sacred Diminutive (fuur')**
`fuur'[concept]` = something small that carries sacred weight.
`fuur'kohr` = a small choice that matters disproportionately.
`fuur'Skaer` = a fragment of god (not lesser — small but complete, like a shard of mirror).
`fuur'vinr` = a small mark (a Falna so faint it shouldn't exist — and yet).

**Rule 15 — Temporal Marker ('vel suffix)**
`[Root]'vel` = "in the time of / during the reign of."
`Haitt'vel` = during wartime.
`Ulfkon'ur'vel` = during the Wolf-Chosen era.
`Vel'ith'vel` = during the Time of the First Stillness (before the Aleabishal).

### TIER 3 — TENSE & ASPECT (Rules 16–17)

**Rule 16 — Past Completive ('ith)**
`[Root]'ith` = completed and sealed; done and no longer becoming.
`haitt'ith` = the war that ended (not the war that is).
`kohr'ith` = the choice that was made (and cannot be unmade).
`jivtal'ith` = the life that ended (the particular death of one person).
Related root: `ith` (void/stillness) = what remains after completion.

**Rule 17 — Ongoing Continuous ('naa)**
`[Root]'naa` = still happening, still becoming, still giving birth to itself.
`haitt'naa` = the war that continues.
`aleabishal'naa` = the ongoing tournament (the Aleabishal that has not yet ended).
`vel'kohr'naa` = the ongoing refusal (a slow, permanent no).
Note: `'naa` draws from `naa` (mother/origin) — the thing that is still giving birth.

### TIER 4 — PERSON & POSSESSION (Rules 18–20)

**Rule 18 — Self-Referential ('me suffix)**
`[Root]'me` = "of/from the self"; the root as it belongs to the speaker.
`kohr'me` = my own choice (not imposed by law or patron).
`vinr'me` = the mark that is mine (not given — earned through survival).
`jivtal'me` = my life (not assigned — chosen).
This suffix carries the heaviest weight in Máalkohr. A Wilak saying `kohr'me` is making a political statement.

**Rule 19 — Collective Possession ('ur)**
`[Root]'ur` = belonging to a collective.
`ulf'ur` = of the wolf-pack.
`Ulfkon'ur` = wolf-chosen-collective (already established).
`wilak'ur` = of the human-kin (the last remaining collective).

**Rule 20 — Interrogative Form (vel'?)**
`vel'[concept]?` = rhetorical challenge; "is it not [concept]?"
`Vel'kohr'me?` = "Was this truly your own choice?"
Used in boss dialogue as implicit accusation. The question is never answered in Máalkohr — asking it is the point.

### TIER 5 — COMPARISON & INTENSITY (Rules 21–23)

**Rule 21 — Augmentative (lox' prefix)**
`lox'[concept]` = greater than, more than, the leading form.
`lox'haitt` = the greater war (the Aleabishal in its totality).
`lox'kohr` = the ultimate choosing (used only for the Denatus).
`lox'vinr` = the complete thread (a Falna that has never wavered).

**Rule 22 — Superlative (skaer' prefix)**
`skaer'[concept]` = divine/absolute; beyond comparison.
`skaer'kohr` = the absolute choice (the one that cannot be undone by any law).
`skaer'haitt` = divine war (the Aleabishal itself, named by those who feared to name it).
`Skaervox` = skaer + vox (vox = voice/change) = the Absolute-Changer (the god who transcends alignment).

**Rule 23 — Comparative ('biik suffix)**
`[Root]'biik` = stripped to essence; reduced to bare form; the root without comfort.
`kohr'biik` = the bare choice (the choice with nothing left to lose).
`haitt'biik` = stripped war (fighting when all strategy is gone).
`jivtal'biik` = bare life (survival with no story left — the last breath).

### TIER 6 — CAUSALITY & REGISTER (Rules 24–27)

**Rule 24 — Causal Chain ('johr suffix)**
`[Root]'johr` = "grounds in" / "inevitably results in."
`haitt'johr` = war grounds everything (what war produces as aftermath).
`kohr'johr` = choosing grounds (the choice that becomes the foundation).
`naetuk'johr` = what fear produces (the frozen, rooted consequence of dread).

**Rule 25 — Formal/Elder Register (vel[root]' prefix)**
`vel[root]'` = the formal, elder, ceremonial form of the root concept.
`veler'aef` = the formal-captain-of-the-sea (Veler'aef, one of the five Veler deities).
`veljohr` = the formal-earth (how earth is addressed in divine ceremony, not casual speech).
`velkohr` = the formal choosing (how the Denatus ceremony references the act of choice).

**Rule 26 — Whispered/Forbidden (ch' prefix)**
`ch'[root]` = spoken quietly, with fear, or in ceremony only.
`ch'inollu` = the knowledge you speak carefully (knowledge as a dangerous thing).
`ch'íikul` = patron (never spoken aloud in public — too close to the crime).
`ch'alea` = the forbidden dawn (the morning of the day something irrevocable begins).

**Rule 27 — Collective Entity ('ur'naa)**
`[Root]'ur'naa` = a collective that acts as a single origin-force.
`ulfkon'ur'naa` = the wolf-collective as one mother-entity.
`wilak'ur'naa` = the human-collective-as-one (the species as a single body).

### TIER 7 — SPEECH ACTS & PROPHECY (Rules 28–30)

**Rule 28 — Command Form (VEL'! prefix)**
`VEL'[concept]` = a divine command; the captain commands this to exist or cease.
Written in caps in formal Máalkohr.
`VEL'KOHR` = CHOOSE (the command Skaervox issues at Floor 100).
`VEL'HAITT` = MAKE WAR (divine war-declaration).
`VEL'ITH` = BE STILL (the command that ends things).

**Rule 29 — Prophetic Compound (skaer'[word]'wilak)**
The only grammatically valid triple-compound in Máalkohr.
`skaer'[concept]'wilak` = "the divine-[concept]-human" = something grammatically possible that the world refuses to acknowledge.
The only attested use: `Skaer'kohr'wilak` = The God-Choice-Human.
The divine record writes this and then stops. Rule 30 applies.

**Rule 30 — Silence Marker (— dash)**
A dash after a Máalkohr word means the speaker stopped.
Stopping mid-sentence in divine law = admission of something the law cannot contain.
`kohr'feli—` = the Denatus record that writes "Chosen False" and cannot complete the sentence.
`Skaer'kohr'wilak—` = the title the divine record writes and refuses to acknowledge.
The dash is not punctuation. It is evidence.
