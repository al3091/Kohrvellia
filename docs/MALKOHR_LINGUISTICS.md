### 2. The Apostrophe is Grammar, Not Style

Every `'` in the language is a **morpheme boundary marker** — a semantic hinge between two concepts.

| Word | Parse | Meaning |
|------|-------|---------|
| `naa'` | naa + ' | Mother (divine breath = sacred feminine root) |
| `Apsa'` | Apsa + ' | Water God (water + sacred breath) |
| `Johr'ubi` | Johr + 'ubi | Earth + Bird = Bird Continent |
| `Veler'johr` | Veler + 'johr | Captain + Earth = Earth Captain |
| `Naa'sayab` | naa' + sayab | Mother + [sayab] = Quori deity |
| `Haitt'ok` | Haitt + 'ok | War + agent suffix = Warrior |
| `yu'umna` | yu + 'umna | [yu] + Father-form = Father |
| `k'agla` | k' + agla | Glottal + fire-body = Fire |
| `k'oer` | k' + oer | Glottal + forest-body = Forest |

**Rule:** `[Root]'[Modifier]` = compound meaning, with the apostrophe always marking a semantic join.

**Implication for content generation:** Any new compound word in Malkohr should use `'` as the join. Never `·`, `-`, or concatenation without it.

## II. COMPLETE GRAMMAR RULES

### Rule 1 — Compound Formation
```
[Root1] + ' + [Root2-abbreviated] → Compound meaning
```
Abbreviation: drop the final vowel of Root2 if Root2 ends in a vowel + consonant.

### Rule 2 — Body Extension (-me)
```
[BodyPart] + me → "that which extends from [BodyPart]"
```

### Rule 3 — Agent Creation (-ok)
```
[Verb/Noun] + 'ok → "one who [does/embodies] the root"
```

### Rule 4 — Place Suffix (-ia)
```
[Concept] + vellia/-ia → "land/place of [concept]"
```
Examples: Kohrvellia (land of choosing), Haitt'ia (land of war), Nolu'ia (land of knowledge)

### Rule 5 — Divine Fusion (God Name Formation)
```
[Concept1-truncated] + [Concept2-modified] + ['suffix] → Divine name
```
- Ulf (wolf) + kon (chosen) + 'ur = Ulfkon'ur
- Uba (bird-form) + 'niyata (death-suffix) = Uba'niyata
- Kuun (serpent) + kalin (multi-head?) + 'ah (head-suffix) = Kuukalin'ah

### Rule 6 — Teen/Dozen Numbers (-ki)
```
[1-9] + ki → "10 + [number]"
[Number] + cois → "[number] × 10"
```

### Rule 7 — Phonological Alignment Markers
| Initial Sound | Typical Alignment | Semantic Domain |
|---------------|------------------|----------------|
| `V-` | NG/CG | Body, institution, governance |
| `k'-` | LG or CE (extremes) | Sacred force (holy or destructive) |
| `ch'-` | LN/CN | Cultural identity, race, method |
| `naa'` | — | Divine maternal (sacred prefix) |
| Short CVC | TN | Numbers, primal elements |
| Long compound | LE/NE | Power, war, suffering |

### The Quori

`QUORI DE BRUJO = Naa'sayab; madre de la naturaleza`

- `Quori` is not a god. It's a **rank** or **class** — "Brujo-Quori" suggests a shamanic title.
- `Naa'sayab` = Mother (naa') + sayab (unknown root) = "Mother of [sayab]"
- She is *madre de la naturaleza* — Mother of Nature, separate from the Greater Gods
- Hypothesis: The Quori are divine intermediaries — mortals who achieved god-adjacency through the natural world. Naa'sayab is the only known Quori.
- `sayab` may be the Malkohr word for *"the wild"* or *"the unnamed green."*

### The World's Moral Philosophy (From the Alignment Map)

The Malkohr language encodes a coherent (if dark) moral worldview:

- **The natural world is righteous** (Forest, Animal, Beast = LG). Nature follows ancient law.
- **Human institutions are corrupt** (Government, Village = NE). Civilization produces neutral harm.
- **Love is manipulation** (Love = NE). Emotion without structure causes predictable damage.
- **Family is the root of violence** (Family = CE). Tribal bonds are the world's most chaotic evil.
- **Killing with purpose is holy** (Assassin = LG). Death by intention is more righteous than death by war (War = LE).
- **The sky is good but unconstrained** (Sky = CG). Freedom above is chaotic good — worth having, hard to govern.
- **Knowledge is neutral power** (Knowledge = LN). Information without morality is just law.
- **Fire is evil** (Fire = CE). The most primal destructive force is the world's most dangerous chaos.

**The overarching thesis of Malkohr cosmology:**
> *The natural world is ordered and righteous. The world of men is ordered toward evil. The only escape from institutional corruption is to return to the forest, or become the assassin — the one who kills with purpose instead of politics.*

### Grammar Rule 8 — The Sacred vs Mortal Plural

Two distinct plural morphemes exist:

**Mortal plural: root + `e` (no apostrophe)**
- Wilak → Wilake (humans)
- venoium → venoiume (champions when referenced as mortal agents)

**Divine/sacred plural: root + `'e` (with apostrophe)**
- Skaer → Skaer'e (the gods)
- venoium → venoium'e (champions when referenced as divine-connected)
- ch'íikul → ch'íikul'e (patrons)

*The same word (venoium) can take EITHER plural depending on context* — when champions are discussed as mortal servants, they get `-e`; when discussed as divine instruments, they get `-'e`. This is the deepest grammatical encoding of the game's central tension: are the champions mortal or divine? The answer changes based on who's speaking and why.

### Grammar Rule 9 — The Ordinal Number Prefix (Championship Titles)
