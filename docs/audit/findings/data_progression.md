## Unit W3-DPr2 — `data/achievements/level{4,5,6}` (targetLevels 5–7) — Session 44 — audited first-hand (voice pass; data validated in DPr1)

**Context:** the mid-tier achievements. The registry-wide data validation was done in DPr1; this unit is the

### Findings

**KV-AUD-329 | S3 | Confirmed (data) / Likely (runtime) | System achievements | L1/L9 | `Achievement.ts:44,174-176` + `data/achievements/*` (9 entries)** — **The 9 "conduct/constraint" achievements (`comparison: 'eq'`×6 + `'lt'`×3) are broken by monotonic, cumulative counters — a *third* uncompletable vector, on otherwise-live requirement types.** `isRequirementMet` **does** implement the comparison operators correctly (`eq`/`gte`/`lte`/`gt`/`lt`, `:174-176` — a real positive), so the defect is not "comparison unhandled." The problem is the **interaction with the progress model**: the counters these compare against (`damage_taken`, `kill_count`) are **monotonic and lifetime-cumulative** (W1-S6b `Math.max`/`+amount`), while the achievements intend a **scoped conduct condition**. Concretely: **"Untouchable Champion"** ("Defeat 2 bosses without taking any damage from them") is `boss_kill:2` + `damage_taken value:0 comparison:'eq'` — but `damage_taken` is the **cumulative total**, so `eq:0` actually demands *never taking a single point of damage the entire game*, not "no boss damage" (effectively impossible, and not the stated condition); and the `'lt'` constraints (**"Undamaged Depths"** "less than 100 damage on floors 5-8", **"Speedrunner"** "less than 20 kills") are **true at progress=0** and silently flip to false as the cumulative counter climbs, with no per-floor/run scope to bound them. So this whole class of "no-damage / speedrun / flawless" conduct achievements — the *aspirational* high-skill content — does not track its intended condition. (Distinct from 325's dead types and 326's undiscoverable tier: these use **live** types but are defeated by counter scope.) → scope the counters per the achievement window (run/floor/boss) or add a constraint-evaluator that snapshots at the scope boundary. *Repro:* take any damage early in a run, then defeat 2 bosses flawlessly → "Untouchable Champion" never fires (cumulative `damage_taken` ≠ 0). *Fix-effort:* medium. *Verification: read (`isRequirementMet`) + data.* *Pattern: P10 (semantics mismatch — operator honored, scope not).* 

**KV-AUD-330 | S4 | Confirmed | System achievements | L7 | `stat_reach` usages in level{4,5}** — **`stat_reach` requirement value is semantically conflated — same value, two meanings.** `stat_reach value:4` is documented as **"Grade D in 4 different stats"** in `l4` "Balanced Growth" but as **"all 8 stats at Grade D"** in `l5` "Balanced Warrior," while `value:5` is **"Reach Grade C"** in `l4` "Stat Excellence" — so the `value` field is used as a **grade index** (4=D, 5=C) in some entries and an implied **stat-count** in others. Whatever the store's single interpretation of `updateProgress('stat_reach', value)` is, at least one of these descriptions is wrong/unreachable. → split into explicit `{ grade; count }` or two requirement types. *Fix-effort:* low. *Pattern: P4 (overloaded stringly/numeric field).*
