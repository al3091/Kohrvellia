## Unit W3-DC1 — `data/monsters/baseMonsters.ts` (1231 lines) — Session 36 — audited first-hand

**Context:** the bestiary spine — **36 base monsters** (CR **0.5** `RAT_SWARM` → **16.0** `PRIMORDIAL`),
**7 prefixes** (weak/young/fierce/armored/elite/ancient/mythic), **5 suffixes** (of_flame/of_frost/of_venom/
the_swift/the_undying), and 3 selection utilities. The **live** monster-generation entry is
`getRandomMonsterForFloor`, called by `useCombatStore.ts:269,315` (grep-confirmed). All 36 entries are present
in `ALL_BASE_MONSTERS` (matches CLAUDE.md "36+ monsters").

### Findings

**KV-AUD-298 | S2 | Confirmed (data side of 189/051) | L9 | `baseMonsters.ts` `immunities`/`resistances` across all 36** — **Poison is negated by over half the bestiary → a poison build is non-viable, with no in-game signal.** **17 of 36** base monsters list `immunities:['poison']` (every undead — skeleton/zombie/ghost/wraith/lich/death_knight; every slime/elemental; most demons + dragons — arch_demon/pit_fiend/demon_lord/elder_dragon/void_dragon/ancient_wyrm; titan; primordial), and **2 more resist** it (spider, basilisk) → **~53% of monsters take 0 or reduced poison**. This is the **data side of the 189/051 softlock** (poison vs `armorType` bone/armor/ethereal = 0 dmg): the poison-immune set overlaps almost exactly with bone (skeleton/lich), armor (mimic/giants/titan/behemoth), and ethereal (ghost/fire_elemental/wraith) armor. So a player who specs into poison (a `pierce+poison` spider-style weapon, or the `of_venom` suffix on their own gear) deals **zero** to a huge, *increasing* fraction of the bestiary as they descend (undead/demons/dragons dominate the deep floors) — and nothing surfaces the immunity. Combined with the StatusEffect schism (190), poison-as-status is also dead. → either reduce poison immunity coverage, add an in-combat "Immune" signal, or give poison a guaranteed-chip floor.

**KV-AUD-299 | S3 | Confirmed (compounds 169) | L9/L3 | `baseMonsters.ts:1193-1208` + grep** — **Monster selection is unweighted uniform random, and the `biomes` field is dead.** (a) `getRandomMonsterForFloor` (`:1202-1208`, the live path called at `useCombatStore.ts:269,315`) calls `getMonstersForFloor` — which filters **only** on `minFloor`/`maxFloor` (`:1193-1196`) — then picks with a flat `available[Math.floor(Math.random()*available.length)]` (`:1207`). There is **no CR-proximity weighting**, so within one floor the encounter CR swings wildly: on **floor 10** the eligible pool spans `slime` (CR 0.8) to `dark_knight` (CR 3.5) with **equal probability** (a ~4.4× CR swing), making difficulty a coin-flip rather than a curve. (b) Every monster carries a `biomes: string[]` (e.g. `VOID_DRAGON.biomes=['void']`), but a repo-wide grep for `.biomes` finds **zero readers** in any selection/generation path — `getMonstersForFloor` ignores it. So **biome-gating of monsters is entirely dead** (36 dead arrays + the `Monster.ts:108` field), compounding **169** (the `Colors.biome` `void`/`labyrinth`/`dreamscape` are also dead and `getBiomeForFloor` only makes 8/10). → weight selection by CR distance to the floor's target; either wire biome filtering into `getMonstersForFloor` or delete the `biomes` field.

**KV-AUD-300 | S3 | Confirmed (confirms 165/055) | L9 | `baseMonsters.ts` `xpValue` ×36** — **Every base monster carries a vestigial `xpValue`** (`RAT_SWARM:5` … `PRIMORDIAL:800`). Per the W2-P1a revision (222, revising 055/164/165/203), the only live consumer of `rewards.xp` is the proficiency channel (and it's physical-biased); the `xpValue` as a *reward* is a remnant of the abandoned pre-"no XP bar" design. So all 36 values are dead-or-vestigial data on the canonical reward path. → remove `xpValue` from `BaseMonster` (and the bosses) or formally repurpose it as the proficiency yield (and document that).

**KV-AUD-301 | S3 | Confirmed (confirms 052/168; extends 190) | L9 | `baseMonsters.ts:1087-1137`** — **The modifier set is thin, and its status/passive data is largely dead.** Only **5 suffixes + 7 prefixes** back a 100-floor game. The 3 elemental suffixes attach `statusEffect` data — `of_flame→burn (15%)`, `of_frost→freeze (20%)`, `of_venom→poison (30%)` — but per **052/025** monster status procs derive from the base `damageTypes`, **not** from `MonsterSuffix.statusEffect`, so this suffix-proc data is **dead in combat**; worse, `burn`/`freeze` are not canonical `StatusEffect` ids (the **190** schism), so even if the proc fired there is no handler. The 2 high suffixes attach `passiveAbility` — `the_swift→double_attack_chance`, `the_undying→regeneration` — which need a combat handler that the W2-P1 combat read did not find (likely display-only, the **188** pattern). Net: of the 5 suffixes, the 3 most common contribute only their `bonusDamage`/`crModifier`, and the 2 rare ones are probably cosmetic. → drive procs from suffix data (close 052), reconcile `burn/freeze` to the canonical status set (190), and wire or drop the two passives.

**KV-AUD-302 | Info | Confirmed (discharges 163; queues 166/loot + 162 inputs) | L9 | `baseMonsters.ts` (whole)** — **Cross-check discharges + inputs for downstream units.**
- **163 (Infinity-reward-on-`baseCR:0`) is LATENT, not live:** the minimum `baseCR` is **0.5** (`RAT_SWARM`); the lowest reachable *final* CR is ≈ **0.2** (`RAT_SWARM` + the `weak` prefix, `crModifier:-0.3`). **No base monster — nor any base+prefix combo — reaches CR 0**, so the divide-by-CR Infinity-reward landmine (163/053) is **not exercised by current data** (the code guard is still advisable for robustness).
- **166 (MonsterCategory):** 8 categories are used — `humanoid/undead/beast/elemental/aberration/demon/giant/dragon`. `aberration` is used by **only** `MIMIC`; verify it exists in the `MonsterCategory` union **and** has a matching loot pool (→ W3-DPr5 `lootPools.ts`).
- **18 distinct `lootTable` refs** (`goblin_loot`…`primordial_loot`, `vermin_loot`, `mimic_loot`, `titan_loot`, `primordial_loot`…) — confirm every one resolves in `lootPools.ts` or monsters drop nothing (→ W3-DPr5).
- **162 sim inputs (recorded):** `baseHP` curve runs **35** (`BAT`) → **1000** (`PRIMORDIAL`), roughly tracking CR; the multiplicative scaling stack (level × zone × prefix) is applied at generation (`Monster.ts` / dungeon store), **not** here — so the owed 100-floor sim still needs those multipliers + player damage.
- **Deep-floor variety is thin:** floors **70–100** draw from only ~**7** monsters (`elder_dragon`, `titan`, `demon_lord`, `void_dragon`, `primordial`, `death_knight`, `ancient_wyrm`) — compounding 162 (the deep end is both sparse *and* likely unwinnable).
- **Positives:** the CR↔HP↔attack curve is monotonic and sensible; `weaknesses`/`resistances`/`immunities` are richly and individually specified (10 damage types in play); `armorType` coverage is broad (leather/bone/flesh/ethereal/armor/plate/scales/magic_resistant). The bestiary *data* is the healthiest content layer seen so far — its problems are wiring (biomes/xp/suffix) and balance (poison, flat selection), not malformed entries.

**KV-AUD-303 | S2 | Confirmed via SIMULATION (discharges the owed 162 sim; corrects 162) | L9 | `Monster.ts:255-318,474-499` + `Stats.ts:307-360` + `.audit_tmp/scaling_sim.js`** — **The owed 100-floor scaling sim is executed: the run is unwinnable past ~floor 15 — ~70 floors earlier than 162 estimated.** Using the exact formulas (monster `floor((monsterLevel×levelMult×1.0 + baseHP)×zoneMult)`, player `effStat×0.008 + weaponDmg`, `maxHP = 50 + effEND×0.1 + effSTR×0.02`), monster HP scales ~**670×** and attack ~**770×** over 100 floors while player attack/HP scale only ~**5×** — because **`monsterLevel ≈ floor` (1→100) but the player level is gated to 1→7** (the zone table's `targetPlayerLevel`, `Monster.ts:229-235`). The earlier "3-layer multiplicative HP" framing was imprecise (it's additive-then-zone). **Executed verdict:** playable floors **1–9**; lose-by-attrition from **~floor 10** (die before the kill); **reliable one-shot by ~floor 20** (avg monster attack 129 vs player maxHP 197). At fl.50 attack 1,001 vs HP 365; fl.86 attack 6,052 vs HP 617 → 100–450+ hits to kill while one-shot. **This precisely explains the floor-25 content cutoff.** Robust to the growth/damage assumptions (flagged in-script): the one-shot wall is monster-attack ≫ player-HP, independent of the kill model.
- **Repro / symptom:** descend with any in-design build → by floor ~15–20 a typical monster's attack exceeds your max HP (one/two-shot death) and kills take 15→100+ hits; deeper is arithmetic suicide. Playable window ≈ floors 1–9.
- **Fix-effort:** **High · blast-radius wide** (the scaling spine) — gate `monsterLevel` to the *player's* level not the floor, and/or scale player power on floor; flatten the `zoneMultiplier` curve; raise the END→maxHP coefficient. The script is re-runnable and is now the **regression harness** for any re-tune.
- **Method note:** first **executed** finding (vs read-and-reason) — adopting the "execute, don't just read" upgrade.

**Unit verdict (W3-DC1 — `baseMonsters.ts` COMPLETE):** the 36-monster bestiary is well-formed and richly specified, but the screen/engine never uses three of its fields — `biomes` (dead, 299), `xpValue` (vestigial, 300), and suffix `statusEffect`/`passiveAbility` (dead per 052/188, 301) — and its one genuine balance defect is **poison non-viability** (298, the data side of 189). Selection is unweighted uniform random (299). The Infinity-reward landmine (163) is confirmed **latent** (no `baseCR:0`). No new S1/S0. **Next ID 303. NEXT UP = W3-DC2** (`monsters/{monsterArt,monsterFlavor,index}` + `combat/combatNarration`), then W3-DC3 weapons (carries 185/153/119).
