### Findings

**KV-AUD-343 | S1 (discharges + quantifies 099 — stays S1) | Confirmed (sim/import) | System sacred-items | L3/L7 | Prior-ref 099/207/208/210 | `src/data/items/*` + `useSacredItemStore.ts:206-276`** — **The sacred-item reward tier — the game's rarest, most elaborately-authored loot — is multiply dead, now with hard numbers: 244 of 588 items (41%) are flatly unobtainable, all 490 deity relics never reveal, and every passive is decorative.** Importing the real corpus and classifying each item's acquisition against the live-metric set yields three independent locks, any one of which alone neuters the tier:
- **(a) 41% unobtainable by acquisition.** **244 of 588** items have a `requireAll` requirement on a **dead metric** (value > 0) → `evaluateRequirement` returns 0 < value forever. The dead metrics are the 13 hard `return 0` stubs (`boss_noattack`, `boss_skillonly`, `floors_nodeath`, `floor_noconsumable`, `floor_nodefend`, `dodges_total`, `achievements_total`, `level_reached`, `stats_grade`, `same_weapon_run`, `debuffs_active_boss`, `sp_damage_dealt`, `custom`) + 4 **no-writer** metrics (`paragon`, `event_success`, `skill_uses`, `skill_sp_spent` — their writers `recordParagon`/`incrementEventSuccess`/`incrementSkillUse` have **0 callers**, re-confirmed this session). Examples: `deity_zeus_accessory` (`paragon≥1`), `deity_athena_weapon` (`boss_skillonly≥1`), `deity_aphrodite_accessory` (`stats_grade≥1`), `deity_malik_weapon` (`skill_uses≥1`), `deity_hecate_weapon` (`skill_uses≥100`).
- **(b) 15 false-unlocks.** 15 items carry a `value:0` "must-be-zero" requirement on a **dead** metric — `checkItemUnlockable` evaluates `evaluateRequirement(...) === 0`, and a dead metric *always* returns 0, so the intended challenge requirement is **auto-satisfied** and the item unlocks on its easier remaining reqs (e.g. `deity_susanoo_weapon` `boss_noattack=0`, `deity_tsukuyomi_weapon` `event_success=0`).
- **(c) The reveal system is 100% dead for the deity tier.** All **490** deity relics are `isSecret:true` + `revealFavorRequired:100`, and `revealFavorRequired` is read by **0 stores** (208) → the hand-written cryptic deity hints are **never shown**; and favor never reaches 100 anyway (the shrine-only favor model, 270/121→242). So even the ~246 *acquisition-reachable* deity relics are functionally unreachable — the player is **never told the condition** and never crosses the reveal gate.
- **(d) Every passive is decorative.** All **588** items carry a `passiveId` + `passiveDescription`, but **no passive-resolution engine exists** (210 confirmed): `passiveId` has 0 consumers, and `passiveDescription` is only *copied into a display field* — `sacredItemConversion.ts:61` sets `specialMechanic: item.passiveDescription`, and `specialMechanic` is itself **dead** (`311`, no reader). So even an obtained relic's signature power does nothing.

**Net for 099:** the discharge keeps it **S1** and *reinforces* it — the original framing (14 dead metrics) understated the scope. The reward tier is dead on **three** axes at once (41% unobtainable · reveal never fires · passives decorative), which is the textbook "complete, elaborate system that is materially unwired" — at the largest scale in the game (588 items). *Repro:* reach 100% favor with any deity → the relic hint never appears; meet any dead-metric condition → the relic never unlocks; equip a relic obtained via a live condition → its passive does nothing. *Fix-effort:* high · blast-radius wide (implement the ~17 missing metric trackers + the reveal gate + a passive resolver — the same effect-resolver seam P1 keeps demanding). *Verification: sim (imported corpus) + grep (writers/resolver).* *Pattern: P1 (modeled+authored, never wired) — the largest instance.* 

**KV-AUD-344 | S3 | Confirmed (quantifies 206) | System sacred-items | L7 | `deityRelics_*.ts` weaponStats** — **107 relic weapons use a non-canonical `damageType`.** The relic `weaponStats.damageType` values are `{physical, dark, magic, holy, chaos}`; **`'physical'` and `'chaos'` are not members of the canonical `DamageType` union** (`slash|pierce|blunt|magic|holy|fire|ice|lightning|poison|dark`, `Weapon.ts:44-54`) — **107 of the 245 relic weapons** use one of them. This is the data-side confirmation + count of W1-T6 **206** (which flagged the divergence at the type level). Currently cosmetic because the weapon triangle is unimplemented (CLAUDE gap #2), but it becomes a real bug the instant the triangle is wired, and `sacredItemConversion.ts:45` already one-way-maps `chaos→dark`, losing the distinction. → add `physical`/`chaos` to the canonical union or normalize at authoring. *Verification: sim.* *Pattern: P4 (stringly-typed enum drift).* 

**KV-AUD-345 | S3 | Confirmed | System sacred-items | L1/L7 | `deityRelics_*.ts` acquisition + `useSacredItemStore:194-255`** — **Relic acquisition conditions are richer than the metrics can express — the `targetType` nuance is silently dropped.** Many relics specify a *qualified* condition via a free-text `targetType` that the evaluator ignores: `deity_hades_weapon` wants `boss_kills value:5 targetType:'different_characters_floor25'` ("5 *different* characters"), `deity_hades_accessory` wants `consecutive_fights targetType:'player_initiates_elite'`, Poseidon wants flee-then-return. But `evaluateRequirement` reads the **bare numeric metric** (e.g. `case 'boss_kills': return m.boss_kills`) and **drops the `targetType`** for most metrics (only `kills_with_stat`/`kills_type`/`taunt_total` index by it). So an item either unlocks on a **cruder** condition than its text states (any 5 boss kills, not 5 *different-character* kills) or — where the metric is a `simplified proxy` (e.g. `consecutive_fights → m.run_kills`, `:214`) — on something unrelated. The `targetType` is also stringly-typed with no enum (207). → constrain `targetType` to a typed set and have the evaluator honor it, or simplify the conditions to what the metrics track. *Verification: read.* *Pattern: P4/P1 (data declares more than the runtime evaluates).*
